## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts have the highest churn.
- Two-Year contract customers show the highest retention.
- Customers using Electronic Check are more likely to churn.
- Long-term customers are more loyal.
- Automatic payment methods are associated with better retention.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to choose long-term contracts.
- Promote automatic payment methods through incentives.
- Improve customer onboarding.
- Introduce loyalty and reward programs.
- Monitor high-risk customer segments.
- Collect customer feedback regularly.
